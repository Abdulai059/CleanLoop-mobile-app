2.0 - Objectives

> **Purpose:** Define why this product exists, the business outcomes it should achieve, and the value it delivers to users.

---

# 2.1 Objective Overview

The objective of CleanLoop is to build a digital platform that increases the recovery of single-use plastics through incentives, community participation, and partnerships with recycling companies.

The MVP focuses on validating the core operating model rather than maximizing scale. Success will be measured by whether users adopt the platform, Recovery Hubs can efficiently manage operations, and recyclers find value in purchasing verified plastic inventory.

---

# 2.2 Business Objectives

These objectives define the business outcomes CleanLoop aims to achieve during the MVP and pilot.

| ID    | Objective                                         | Success Indicator                                                               |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BO-01 | Validate the end-to-end circular economy workflow | Complete recovery → reward → recycler purchase flow                             |
| BO-02 | Acquire the first recycling partner               | At least one active recycler using the platform                                 |
| BO-03 | Validate the reward economy                       | Users consistently redeem rewards and return to recover more plastic            |
| BO-04 | Build operational processes for Recovery Hubs     | Recovery Hubs successfully verify and manage inventory digitally                |
| BO-05 | Demonstrate measurable environmental impact       | Verified plastic recovery data available through dashboards                     |
| BO-06 | Establish the foundation for future monetization  | Identify viable revenue opportunities from recyclers and institutional partners |

# 2.3 User Objectives

## Households

### Goal

Recover plastic in exchange for useful rewards.

### Desired Outcome

Users should feel that recycling is simple, rewarding, and worthwhile.

Success means:

- Registration takes only a few minutes.
- Recovery is straightforward.
- Points are awarded immediately after verification.
- Rewards are easy to redeem.
- Users are motivated to participate repeatedly.

---

## Schools

### Goal

Encourage students to participate in environmental activities through organized recycling campaigns.

### Desired Outcome

Schools can monitor participation, compete with other schools, and earn educational rewards that benefit students.

---

## Recovery Agents

### Goal

Digitally verify recovered plastic quickly and accurately.

### Desired Outcome

Agents can process recoveries with minimal manual paperwork while maintaining accurate inventory records.

---

## Recycling Companies

### Goal

Access reliable supplies of verified recyclable materials.

### Desired Outcome

Recyclers spend less time sourcing materials and have greater confidence in inventory quality and traceability.

---

## Administrators

### Goal

Monitor platform performance and manage operations efficiently.

### Desired Outcome

Administrators can oversee users, Recovery Hubs, campaigns, rewards, inventory, and analytics from a centralized dashboard.

---

# 2.4 Product Objectives

The MVP should achieve the following product goals.

### Objective 1 — Digital Registration

Enable households, schools, and Recovery Agents to register and access the platform securely.

---

### Objective 2 — Recovery Verification

Digitize the process of receiving, weighing, verifying, and recording plastic recoveries.

---

### Objective 3 — Digital Rewards

Automatically calculate and award CleanPoints based on verified recoveries.

---

### Objective 4 — Reward Redemption

Allow users to exchange accumulated CleanPoints for approved rewards.

---

### Objective 5 — Inventory Management

Maintain accurate inventory records at each Recovery Hub for recycler collection.

---

### Objective 6 — Community Engagement

Encourage participation through campaigns, competitions, and leaderboards.

---

### Objective 7 — Reporting

Generate dashboards and reports for operational monitoring and impact measurement.

---

# 2.5 Non-Objectives (Out of Scope)

To ensure focus, the following capabilities are intentionally excluded from the MVP.

### Artificial Intelligence

- Automatic waste recognition.
- Predictive collection optimization.

---

### Financial Services

- Loans.
- Savings products.
- Mobile money wallet integration beyond reward redemption.

---

### Logistics

- Fleet management.
- Driver routing optimization.

---

### Carbon Markets

- Carbon credit generation.
- Carbon offset trading.

---

### Advanced Analytics

- Predictive demand forecasting.
- Machine learning recommendations.

These features may be explored after successful validation of the core platform.

---

# 2.6 Success Criteria

The objectives of the MVP will be considered achieved when the platform demonstrates:

### Operational Success

- Users can complete the full recovery journey.
- Recovery Agents successfully verify recoveries.
- Inventory records remain accurate.
- Rewards are issued correctly.

### User Success

- Users understand how to use the platform without extensive training.
- Participants return for additional recoveries.
- Schools actively engage in campaigns.

### Business Success

- At least one recycler participates in the workflow.
- Recovery Hubs successfully operate using digital processes.
- Stakeholders express interest in continuing beyond the pilot.

# 2.7 Product Principles

The following principles guide all product decisions.

### User-Centered

Every feature should reduce friction and improve the user experience.

---

### Mobile First

The primary experience is designed for mobile devices, with web dashboards supporting operational users.

---

### Inclusive

Feature phone users must be able to participate through USSD.

---

### Transparent

Every recovery, reward, and inventory transaction must be traceable and auditable.

---

### Scalable

The architecture should support future expansion without requiring major redesign.

---

### Data-Driven

Product improvements should be based on measurable user behavior and operational insights.
